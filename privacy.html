Hermes privacy policy

Hermes is used only by its owner, Nicholas Gigliotti. It accesses his own Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts) solely to act on his requests.

This data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
